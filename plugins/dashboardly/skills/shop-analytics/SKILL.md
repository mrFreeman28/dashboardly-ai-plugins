# Shop Sales, Profit & Advertising

Read [analysis rules](../../references/analysis-rules.md). This skill reports connected shop facts returned by Dashboardly. Hypothetical offers and user-supplied costs belong to the separate offer-modeling workflow.

1. Call `get_connection_context` to establish the current connection and effective capabilities. Use `list_shops` to resolve the authorized shop; name a sole available shop, or ask for an explicit choice when several are plausible. Require `financial_summary` for summaries, `profit_loss` for P&L/daily reads and `advertising` for ads, as returned for that exact shop. Capability `ready` means callable and authorized, not fresh, complete or final source data. An installed skill or supplied shop ID does not grant access. Stop the affected operation when its capability is unavailable; do not use another connection or a legacy endpoint.
2. Establish the reporting dates. If they are ambiguous, ask only for the missing period. Use the current schemas: financial reads take `shopId`, `period` with `startDate`/`endDate`, and optionally `settings.includeSampleCosts`. Summary periods are limited to 366 inclusive days. The P&L tool also requires `granularity` (`day`, `week`, `month`, `quarter` or `year`): day periods are limited to 90 days, week periods to 184 days and coarser periods to 366 days. Daily financial trends are limited to 90 days. Do not silently shorten a requested range or invent a caller timezone, reporting basis, merchant or settings override.
3. Call `get_dashboard_summary` for the shop's canonical sales/profit summary. Use `get_profit_loss` for its P&L breakdown and `get_daily_financials` only when a daily trend is needed. Use only returned values, units, evidence and metric definitions. Daily results declare metadata once in `metricDefinitions`; each day’s `values` array follows that exact order. Match by position, then show the metric name and unit. A null cell is unavailable source data, while `"0"` is a measured zero; never shift cells or fill gaps. Whole-range `totals` are separate canonical values, not sums of displayed days. Do not rebuild profit from order rows or model arithmetic. Keep GMV, product sales, refunds, revenue and profit distinct. P&L reference/subtotal rows must not be summed as independent expenses. Preserve the server's expense signs and canonical totals.
4. Treat response context as authoritative: selected shop, currency, actual dates, effective timezone, saved/applied reporting settings, fallback reasons and definition version. A requested setting can differ from what the source applied. Show that difference instead of relabeling the result. Do not compare periods with incompatible settings, currencies, populations or definitions, or manufacture deltas that the backend did not support.
5. For advertising, call `get_advertising_performance` with `shopId`, `period` of at most 90 days, and optional bounded `page`/`limit` (limit at most 100). It reads imported campaign facts and must not schedule imports, change campaigns or fetch live TikTok reports. Follow its returned advertiser reporting calendar and verified currency; these need not match the financial report's shop calendar. Do not merge incompatible calendars or currencies.
6. Keep advertising bases explicit. Campaign gross spend is before platform credits; campaign-attributed revenue is not total shop sales and must not be added to it. The financial summary's net/cash ad-spend basis is a separate source. Campaign rows or their summary do not prove whole-shop billed spend. TikTok's revenue/cost ratio is ROAS, not traditional profit ROI. Use returned canonical ratios and their units; do not average ratios, recompute a missing denominator as zero, or divide blended attributed orders by paid clicks and call that paid conversion.
7. Read quality before making a conclusion. Missing costs, nullable metrics, daily gaps, unknown/mixed currency, stale imported facts and absent attribution remain explicit. `unavailable` is not zero; `preparing` is not a completed report. Response generation/retrieval time is not a proved source watermark. Do not claim current, settled or final data unless the returned source evidence establishes it. A partial result can support only its available facts; incomplete costs do not prove full profitability.
8. Return a concise answer in the user's language: selected shop and reporting context; a table of supported sales/profit/ad metrics; the requested breakdown or trend; evidence and material gaps. Attribute observations to the returned data and label causal explanations as hypotheses. Do not promise profit, measured conversion uplift, exact advertising billing or full business completeness from a partial projection.

These are read-only aggregate reports. Do not request or expose buyer contacts, addresses, tracking, order-level personal data, tokens or credentials. Do not change costs, orders, listings, ads or account access, initiate imports, send/save/publish a report, or make purchases. An expired connection uses the host's supported reconnect flow; a permission or source-quality refusal is not permission to broaden access.
